Who I Am

I'm Yajas Mishra, a student at Podar International School, Indore with too many interests and not enough hours in the day. I sketch comic strips, train in kickboxing, learn keyboard, and somehow manage to build school projects that look nothing like school projects.

This site is my space — part portfolio, part creative dump, part proof that I actually did my homework. Everything you see here is made by me.
